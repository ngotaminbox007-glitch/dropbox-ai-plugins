Mission Control — Stage 3 Rules
================================

Source of truth = data/decisions.csv (plus live queue).
Chat remains coordination only.

Live items: Active, Waiting, Needs Approval → data/live_decisions.csv
Stale items → data/stale_review.csv (archive or reactivate)

No office may send, pay, delete, share private info, or file/government-file without approval.
One lead office and one support office per item.
Only the lead office tells the user.
Drafts are not filings.
No office crosses its lane.
Max 7 approvals per day (data/approvals.csv).
If an issue loops 3 times, escalate (data/loop_alerts.csv).
Calendar + alarms are the reminder system (data/reminder_log.csv).
Every action is audited (data/audit_log.csv).

Stage 3 may: intake, classify, route, validate, remind, summarize, queue, alert.
Stage 3 may not execute high-risk actions without an approval record.
